WHO WE ARE

Your Everyday Essentials.

Hussam Stationary is built around one simple idea — making everyday shopping easier, convenient and reliable for the local community.

From school notebooks and pens to office supplies, art materials and educational essentials, we provide products that students, families and professionals use every day.

But Hussam is not limited to stationery. Our store also brings together a selection of daily groceries, edible items, household essentials and other useful products under one roof.

Whether you are preparing for school, working in an office, shopping for your home or simply looking for something you need today — Hussam aims to make the experience simple and convenient.
